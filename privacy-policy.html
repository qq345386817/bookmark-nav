Bookmark Nav Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 19, 2026

Summary

Bookmark Nav does not collect, upload, sell, rent, transfer, or share your bookmarks or personal information. Your bookmark data stays in your browser.

Information We Do Not Collect

Bookmark Nav does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, browsing history, keystrokes, bookmark contents, or other personal information.

Bookmark Data

The extension uses Chrome's bookmarks API to read your bookmark folders and saved links so it can display them in the new tab dashboard. This data is processed locally in Chrome and is not sent to us or to a third-party server.

Local Extension Settings

Bookmark Nav may store local preferences such as theme, last selected view, and expanded folders using Chrome's local extension storage. These settings remain on your device unless Chrome syncs extension data according to your own browser settings.

Analytics and Tracking

Bookmark Nav does not use Google Analytics or any other analytics tracker in the extension. We do not track how you use your bookmarks.

Cookies

The Bookmark Nav extension does not use cookies. This static website does not require cookies for its core pages.

Children's Privacy

Bookmark Nav is a general productivity tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the extension changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when we make material changes.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at cameoshell09@gmail.com.
